  ## *I could write about how vocally gifted Veronika is, how musical she is,
  how she has such a keen sense of both the communicative and sonic dimensions
  of speech, whether she sings in Ukrainian, Czech, or French; about how she is
  not content simply to sing, but searches at the piano or accordion for the
  harmonic architecture and style of the songs she interprets; about how, with
  all these talents, she is hardworking, reliable, and a good colleague. And I
  could write about all this either from an armchair in the audience or from the
  pianist’s chair, accompanying her.*


  ## *But above all of that, I value something even rarer: Veronika knows what she wants, what is and is not right for her; she knows that whatever she may sing will always leave its imprint on her face, in her expression, and in her name. She does not merely concern herself with repertoire; she takes care of her character.*\


  ### \


  — Přemysl Rut, srpen 2026
